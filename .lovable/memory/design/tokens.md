## Theme
- Dark navy: HSL(222, 47%, 6%) background
- Primary: HSL(210, 100%, 55%) — bright blue
- Accent: HSL(195, 100%, 50%) — cyan
- Success: HSL(160, 84%, 39%) — teal green

## Effects
- `.glass` — bg-card/60 backdrop-blur-xl border
- `.glass-hover` — hover glow + border-primary/30
- `.glow-sm/md/lg` — blue glow shadows
- `.gradient-text` — blue-to-cyan gradient text
- `.bg-grid` — subtle grid background
- `.bg-radial-glow` — top radial blue glow

## Animations
- `animate-fade-in-up` — 0.5s translateY(20px)
- `animate-glow-pulse` — pulsing glow shadow
- `animate-float` — 3s gentle float
- `animate-shimmer` — shimmer sweep
- `animate-count-up` — number reveal

## Fonts
- Inter (300-900) for sans
- JetBrains Mono for monospace
